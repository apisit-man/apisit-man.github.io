# IPST Brand Profile (สสวท.)

Official visual identity for **The Institute for the Promotion of Teaching Science and Technology (IPST / สสวท.)**.

## Brand shape

- Clean academic-modern rounded cards (`12 px` radius), IPST Deep Royal Blue (`#003366`) headlines with a Tech Cyan (`#0099DA`) rule underneath, and Vibrant Orange (`#F26522`) or Science Green (`#10B981`) highlights for experiments and discoveries.
- Midnight Navy (`#071E3D`) scene ground.
- Dignified, studio-grade editorial animation aesthetic.
