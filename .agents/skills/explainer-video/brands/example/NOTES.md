# Example brand

A neutral profile to copy. Replace the colours in `brand.css`, put your own SVG or PNG into
`images/`, and your licensed fonts into `fonts/`.

## Brand shape

- Rounded rectangle tile, 12 px radius, accent on the dark ground.
- Headline in ExtraBold with a short accent rule under it.
- One logo, one mark. No mascot.

## Files

| File | Used for |
|---|---|
| `brand.css` | every colour and font in the video |
| `images/logo.svg` | opening card, closing slide |
| `images/logo-white.svg` | the corner bug over dark scenes |
| `images/mark.svg` | the large, faint background mark on the cards |
| `fonts/` | loaded with `FontFace` before the first frame |
