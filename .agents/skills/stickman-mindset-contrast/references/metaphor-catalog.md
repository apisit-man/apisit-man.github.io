# High-Converting Mindset Contrast Catalog

Use these proven contrast pairs when creating new split-screen animations. Each concept has a concrete physical metaphor for both the top struggle and bottom ease.

---

## 1. Finance & Wealth Creation

### Pair 1: Active Labor vs. Automated Pipeline (The Classic)
* **Top (Red)**: `Working for Money` / `ทำงานแลกเงิน`
  * *Metaphor*: Carrying heavy buckets labeled "9-TO-5" on a bending pole. The stickman walks tiredly, trips, crashes, and spills all water.
* **Bottom (Green)**: `Building Assets` / `สร้างทรัพย์สิน`
  * *Metaphor*: Reclining reading a book while a permanent pipe labeled "ASSETS" continuously fills a wooden barrel with fresh water.

### Pair 2: Instant Gratification vs. Compounding Investment
* **Top (Red)**: `Buying Liabilities` / `ซื้อหนี้สินอวดคนอื่น`
  * *Metaphor*: Stickman buried under a mountain of luxury shopping bags, car keys, and high-interest credit cards, crushed and suffocating.
* **Bottom (Green)**: `Investing in Compounding` / `ปลูกต้นไม้การเงิน`
  * *Metaphor*: Stickman gently watering a small sapling that has gold coin fruits multiplying under bright sunshine.

### Pair 3: Trading Hours for Cash vs. Scalable Digital Systems
* **Top (Red)**: `Chasing the Clock` / `วิ่งไล่ตามเวลา`
  * *Metaphor*: Running frantically on an endless hamster wheel attached to a giant clock, sweating and panting without going anywhere.
* **Bottom (Green)**: `Leveraged Code & Media` / `ใช้พลังทวีคูณ (Leverage)`
  * *Metaphor*: Stickman asleep peacefully in a hammock under coconut trees while automated gears and robot arms pack boxes in the background.

---

## 2. Productivity & Deep Work

### Pair 4: Endless Cheap Dopamine vs. Monastic Deep Focus
* **Top (Red)**: `Brain Fog & Doomscrolling` / `ติดกับดักโซเชียล`
  * *Metaphor*: Eyes glued to a glowing smartphone, surrounded by swirling notification ghosts (TikTok, Reels, Alerts) while papers and clocks rot away.
* **Bottom (Green)**: `2 Hours of Deep Work` / `โฟกัสสร้างผลงานชิ้นเอก`
  * *Metaphor*: Sitting at a minimalist clean wooden desk with a single steaming cup of coffee and notebook, building a masterpiece brick by brick.

---

## 3. Health & Life Longevity

### Pair 5: Stress, Fast Food & Burnout vs. Vitality & Daily Habits
* **Top (Red)**: `Borrowing Tomorrow's Health` / `ทำลายสุขภาพแลกงาน`
  * *Metaphor*: Slumped over an office desk with empty energy drink cans, fast food boxes, hunched spine, and red bloodshot eyes.
* **Bottom (Green)**: `Nourishing Body & Mind` / `ดูแลสุขภาพเพื่อระยะยาว`
  * *Metaphor*: Jogging gracefully on a serene mountain trail at sunrise, smiling with energetic posture and deep breathing.
