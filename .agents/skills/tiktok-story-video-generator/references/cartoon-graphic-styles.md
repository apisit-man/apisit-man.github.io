# Cartoon Graphic & Subtitle Design Standards

To make vertical TikTok stories feel professionally illustrated and animated, captions and graphic accents must abandon generic video subtitle templates and adopt **Comic/Cartoon Webtoon Aesthetics**.

---

## 🎨 The 4 Pillars of Professional Cartoon Graphics

### 1. Bubbly Comic Speech Cards (Not Plain Text Boxes)
* **Surface:** Warm off-white / Cream `#FFFEFA` with a subtle alpha (`245/255`).
* **Border:** Strong `4px` dark slate outline (`#1E293B`) to establish a classic cartoon cel-shading look.
* **Corner Radius:** `28px` to `32px` for an approachable, organic feel.
* **Drop Shadow:** Soft cartoon shadow offset (`+6px, +8px`, black alpha `60/255`), creating depth without muddying the background illustration.

### 2. Floating Header & Speaker Badges
Every speech card features an attached capsule badge anchored on the top border:
* **Bobby Badge (`bobby`):** Electric Sky Blue (`#0284C7`)
* **Mr. Marsh Badge (`marsh`):** Calming Emerald Teal (`#0D9488`)
* **Tip / Secret Badge (`tip`):** Warm Amber Gold (`#D97706`)
* **Alarm / Struggle Badge (`alert`):** Rose Crimson (`#E11D48`)
* **Focus Badge (`focus`):** Fresh Mint Green (`#10B981`)

### 3. Decorative Emotion Accents
* **Top Episode Pill:** Minimal clean episode index tag (`EPISODE • SCENE 1`) in top safe margin.
* **Sparkle Accents (`✨`):** Dual-layer glowing stars (`#FEF08A` outer, `#FFFFFF` core) placed in resolution/action/outro scenes (Cuts 4, 5, 6).
* **Panic / Vibration Indicators:** Comic emphasis lines for cuts 1 and 2.

### 4. TikTok Safe Zone Compliance
* **Bottom Offset:** Top of the card rests at `y = 1380`, bottom ends around `y = 1570`.
* This ensures that TikTok user handles, music titles, and swipe-up buttons never obscure the dialogue or badges.
