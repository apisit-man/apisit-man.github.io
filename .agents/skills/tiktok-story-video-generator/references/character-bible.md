# Character Bible & Prompt Consistency Guide

To maintain visual continuity across all 6 cuts generated by AI image tools, prompt descriptors must remain rigorously anchored.

---

## 🌟 Primary Duo: Bobby & Mr. Marsh

### 1. บ๊อบบี้ (Bobby)
* **Silhouette:** Chibi boy with a large round head, dark messy hair, expressive anime eyes.
* **Wardrobe:** Oversized cozy mint-green hoodie (`mint-green oversized hoodie`), dark cozy shorts or sweatpants.
* **Personality:** Relatable, slightly anxious, earnest, prone to procrastination but loves learning.
* **Prompt Anchor:**
  `chibi boy Bobby with round head and messy dark brown hair, wearing oversized cozy mint-green hoodie, cute 2D minimal flat pastel aesthetic, clean soft line art --ar 9:16`

### 2. คุณมาร์ช (Mr. Marsh)
* **Silhouette:** Soft floating white marshmallow creature, tiny dark dot eyes, warm blushing pink cheeks.
* **Personality:** Kind, calming, patient, never judges, offers warm re-framing.
* **Prompt Anchor:**
  `small cute floating white marshmallow creature Mr. Marsh with tiny dot eyes and warm blushing cheeks, comforting and empathetic vibe, pastel color palette, soft ambient glow --ar 9:16`

## 🎨 Scene Aesthetic Rules
* **Aspect Ratio:** Strictly `9:16` (`--ar 9:16` for Midjourney/Imagen).
* **Color Palette:** Soft pastel tones (warm cream, soft sage/mint, powder blue, warm honey yellow, gentle terracotta).
* **Lighting:** Cozy ambient warm lighting, clean flat vector / 2D digital illustration style.
* **Framing:** Keep character actions centered in the middle third of the vertical frame to ensure safe margins from TikTok's UI overlays.
