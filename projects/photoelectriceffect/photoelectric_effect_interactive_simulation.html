Photocurrent (I) vs Intensity Directly Proportional

If photon energy E ≥ Φ, increasing light intensity liberates more photoelectrons per second, producing a purely linear increase in current.

The Classical Wave Mystery (1900)

Classical electromagnetic wave theory predicted that increasing wave amplitude (intensity) would deposit more energy into electrons, allowing even low-frequency red light to eventually knock electrons out after a time lag.

Einstein's 1905 Quantum Resolution (Nobel Prize 1921)

Light consists of discrete energy packets called photons, where energy is proportional solely to frequency: E = hf. Photoelectron ejection is a 1-to-1 collision. If a single photon lacks the work function energy (Φ), emission is impossible regardless of how intense the beam is!
